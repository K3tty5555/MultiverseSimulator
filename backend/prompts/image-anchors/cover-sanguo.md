# Universe Cover · 三国乱世（中国山水画）

Traditional Chinese 水墨山水 + subtle color (淡彩山水) painting as a book cover for "Three Kingdoms chaotic era" universe.

## Subject

Epic atmospheric scene: the Yangtze River at the eve of Red Cliffs battle (赤壁). Long narrative scroll composition. Towering cliffs rise on one side, river mist fills the middle. Dragon-prow warships drift on moonlit water, banners catching light. A lone strategist figure (reading as Zhuge Liang silhouette) stands on a cliff observing the fleet. Distant fire glow on far shore. A poem inscription area (leave blank, illegible) upper left.

## Technique

**Traditional Chinese ink landscape painting (山水画)** with restrained color accents:
- Ink wash mountain texture strokes (皴法 - 披麻皴 or 斧劈皴)
- Atmospheric 云雾 mist rendered in wet brush
- Pine trees and rocks with fine brush detail
- Ships outlined in precise linework
- Subtle gold/oxblood color accents only on torches, banners, moon
- Large areas of 留白 (empty space)
- A small vermillion 印章 (red seal) bottom left as is traditional

## Composition

**2:3 portrait orientation, painting fills canvas edge-to-edge**. NO Western frame, NO Art Deco border, NO archival paper edge, NO Western decorative elements. Vertical emphasis (挂轴 hanging-scroll style) with empty upper portion for title breathing room.

## Palette

Ink black dominant. Deep teal-grey river. Oxblood red banners and fire. Tarnished gold moon. Ivory mist. Overall restrained palette typical of Chinese literati landscape.

## Mood

Monumental, tragic, pre-battle tension. Classical Chinese historical landscape feeling (三国演义 mood).

## Negative prompts

NO Western Art Deco frames, NO Art Nouveau borders, NO oval photo frame, NO aged sepia photograph, NO Western archival paper decorative edge, NO realistic photo style, NO 3D render, NO Western fantasy elements. Must be traditional Chinese ink-and-color landscape (山水画) style filling the entire canvas.
