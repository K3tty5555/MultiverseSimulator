# Art Nouveau Pure Corner Ornament (Transparent-ready)

Isolated Art Nouveau corner ornament with MINIMAL background for easy UI compositing.

**Subject**: Single Art Nouveau corner decoration anchored at TOP-LEFT. Flowing organic curves extend from the corner diagonally down-right, featuring: iris flower, acanthus leaves, delicate gold leaf linework, slender scroll-work. Design occupies only the top-left 40% of the canvas; the remaining 60% is PURE SOLID IVORY background (no texture, no scenery, no objects).

**Composition**: 1:1 square. Ornament anchored top-left, extending down and right. Entire background is flat ivory color (#e8dfc8 equivalent) with NO photographs, NO books, NO shelves, NO texture visible.

**Palette**: Tarnished gold linework (primary), deep oxblood accent on iris petals, subtle umber shading. Flat ivory background.

**Mood**: Clean, decorative, ready to be extracted/overlaid on UI.

**Critical negative**: NO background scenery, NO library or shelf behind, NO texture on the background area, NO photos, NO books, NO aged paper effects on the empty area (keep it clean and flat). Only the ornament itself should be detailed — the rest of the canvas is plain solid color.
