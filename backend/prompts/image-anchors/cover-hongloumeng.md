# Universe Cover · 红楼梦（中国院体画）

Traditional Chinese 院体画 (Song/Ming academic court painting) style book cover for 红楼梦 universe.

## Subject

The main archway of 大观园 at dusk. Red lanterns glow on painted beams. Peach blossoms (桃花) drift through the air. Foreground: silhouette of a young scholar (Jia Baoyu) in embroidered robes walking toward the archway, holding an ornate painted fan. Above the archway, dreamy cloud wisps hinting at 太虚幻境. Subtle moon glimpse behind clouds. Garden pavilions and willows in middle ground.

## Technique

**Chinese 工笔 (gongbi) academic painting style**:
- Meticulous linework on architecture (斗拱/雕梁)
- Fine detail on embroidered robes and lanterns
- Atmospheric gongbi color wash in background
- Petals painted individually with delicate brush
- Subtle dream mist rendered in pale wash
- A small 印章 (red seal) in corner

## Composition

**2:3 portrait, painting fills canvas edge-to-edge**. NO Western frame, NO Art Deco, NO archival paper border. Vertical composition with archway central, receding into garden depth.

## Palette

Vermillion (朱砂) lanterns and eave details. Ivory silk robes. Deep teal-indigo evening sky. Peach pink blossoms. Tarnished gold on eaves and beams. Soft misty dusk tones.

## Mood

Dreamy, melancholic, enchanted, on the threshold of illusion. Classical Chinese literary romance (红楼梦) mood.

## Negative prompts

NO Western Art Deco frames, NO Art Nouveau, NO oval photo frame, NO aged sepia photograph, NO archival paper border, NO Western decorative elements, NO anime/manga, NO realistic photo, NO 3D render. Must be Chinese gongbi academic painting style filling entire canvas.
