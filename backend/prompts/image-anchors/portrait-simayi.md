# Character Portrait · 司马懿 (三国杀 CG 风 · 魏)

Paint 司马懿 (Sima Yi), the supreme strategist who outlasted all rivals and whose family founded the Jin dynasty, in the exact same art style as the provided reference images — Chinese heroic collectible card game character art.

## Match the reference style precisely:
- Semi-realistic painterly CG illustration (NOT traditional Chinese ink painting, NOT gongbi, NOT watercolor, NOT anime)
- Bust / upper-body portrait, character fills ~70% of the frame
- 3/4 side view, head slightly turned, deep inscrutable calculating gaze

- Atmospheric themed background (NOT landscape, NOT mountains)
- Fine painterly detail on face, fabric, armor, accessories

## Subject · FROZEN MOMENT

**司马懿** — seated at a strategy table, slowly turning his head to look back over his shoulder.

**CRITICAL FACE**: He is ELDERLY — late 60s to early 70s. Deeply furrowed wrinkled face with heavy crow's feet, pronounced nasolabial folds, sunken hollow cheeks, silver-white hair and beard showing his great age. Age-spotted skin. BUT his narrow deep-set eyes remain piercingly sharp and alive — a very old fox whose body has aged but whose mind is razor-sharp. This contrast (aged body, predatory eyes) is the most important visual element. Face the color of pale parchment, thin lips with a barely perceptible cold smile.

The famous 鹰视狼顾 (wolf-gaze looking back): his body faces forward but his neck is turned sharply, eyes glancing back at an angle that seems almost anatomically extreme — a physical trait historically recorded.

Wearing deep midnight purple and black general's robes with dark gold phoenix embroidery. A dark formal court hat. One hand grips a folded strategy fan, the other rests on the table, expression: terrifying patience of a man who has outlived all rivals.

Atmospheric, not architectural. Deep purple-black dominant, dark gold and cold silver accents, character lit against ominous purple-tinged darkness.

## Mood

"司马仲达，鹰视狼顾" — the wolf in sheep's clothing who endured sixty years to seize everything, the last man standing of the Three Kingdoms era.
