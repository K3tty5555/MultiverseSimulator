# Universe Cover · 明（明代文人画）

Traditional Chinese 明代文人画 (Ming dynasty literati painting) style book cover, centered on 王阳明 at Longchang enlightenment night (龙场悟道).

## Subject

Mountain cave at deep night in 贵州 wilderness. Lone scholar figure (Wang Yangming silhouette) sits in meditation inside a rough stone cave by a small oil lamp. Outside cave mouth: mist-shrouded mountains (贵州群山) under a crescent moon. Wild bamboo stalks lean into the cave opening. An ancient stone stele (石碑) nearby with faint illegible inscription. A subtle golden aura gently surrounds the meditating figure — the moment of enlightenment (致良知).

## Technique

**Ming dynasty literati painting** (沈周/文徵明 lineage):
- Ink-dominant composition
- Restrained color accents
- Meticulous rock texture (皴法 - 披麻皴 for soft mountains)
- Atmospheric mountain mist
- Bamboo rendered in 写意 brush
- Cave mouth as framing device (painted within the picture, NOT as Western frame)
- Subtle golden aura around meditating figure rendered with fine brush gold powder effect
- Small 印章 in corner

## Composition

**2:3 portrait, painting fills canvas edge-to-edge**. NO Western frame, NO Art Deco, NO archival paper border. Cave opening serves as compositional frame for the mountain landscape inside. Meditating figure in lower center.

## Palette

Ink black rock and cave walls. Deep teal-grey night mountain mist. Tarnished gold oil lamp flame + subtle enlightenment aura. Ivory moon. Touch of oxblood on scholar's robe interior. Overall restrained Ming literati palette.

## Mood

Solitary enlightenment, profound philosophical realization, mountain silence. The moment before 心学 was born.

## Negative prompts

NO Western Art Deco frames, NO Art Nouveau, NO oval photo, NO aged sepia photograph, NO archival paper border, NO Western decorative elements, NO realistic photo, NO 3D render, NO anime/manga. Must be Ming-era Chinese literati painting style filling entire canvas.
