# Art Nouveau Corner Ornament

Art Nouveau single-corner ornamental element on aged parchment background.

**Subject**: A decorative corner piece in TOP-LEFT orientation. Flowing organic curves extending down and right from the corner, floral motif (lily or iris), acanthus leaves, gold leaf linework. The rest of the canvas is aged ivory parchment with subtle foxing. The ornament occupies only ~35% of the canvas in the top-left, rest is blank parchment (for compositing into UI).

**Composition**: 1:1 square. Ornament anchored at top-left corner, radiating down-right.

**Palette**: Tarnished gold linework, umber shading, ivory parchment base. No bright colors.

**Mood**: Ornate, classical, Art Nouveau, used for page corners in an antique book.

**Negative**: no readable text, no modern, no 3D render, flat etched look, no watermarks.
