# Art Nouveau Horizontal Border

Art Nouveau horizontal decorative border element on aged parchment background.

**Subject**: A horizontal ornamental frame composed of: flowing organic curves, peacock feather motifs on each end, a central lily or iris floral cluster, gold leaf accents, symmetric composition. Design sits in the center horizontal band; the rest of the canvas is aged ivory parchment with subtle foxing and tobacco yellowing.

**Composition**: 16:9 aspect. Design occupies center 60% of width and center 30% of height. Top and bottom edges of canvas feature slightly darker parchment edges (suggesting the paper is laid on a darker surface).

**Palette**: Tarnished gold for linework, umber and sepia accents on floral shadows, ivory parchment base. No bright or saturated colors.

**Mood**: Ornate, classical, Art Nouveau revival, library archival.

**Negative**: no readable text/letters, no modern, no watermark, no 3D render, must be flat / drawn / etched style.
