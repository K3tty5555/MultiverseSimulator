# Vintage Archival Magical Realism · Global Style Anchor

This style anchor is shared by ALL image generations in LifePlanner multiverse archive.
Concatenate this file first, then a subject-specific prompt.

## Style DNA

**Art movement fusion**: Art Nouveau linework combined with Art Deco geometric accents.

**Core aesthetic**: Vintage Archival Magical Realism —
- sepia-toned archival photography aesthetic
- vintage cinematic film grain and subtle chromatic aberration
- 1920s–1960s European academia atmosphere
- muted warm palette: umber brown, ivory, deep teal, oxblood red, tarnished gold
- soft volumetric light through drifting dust motes
- aged paper texture, subtle foxing stains on edges
- letterboxed composition, cinematic framing
- contemplative, slightly mysterious atmosphere

**Crucial "time-travel feel" recipe** (this is the core magic):
- **Medium layering**: film photo texture + hand-drawn illustration + typewriter text + parchment script appearing together
- **Aging evidence**: faded edges, water stains, tobacco yellowing — as if the image "has been lying for 80 years"
- **Era mismatch**: objects from different decades share the same frame (gramophone beside typewriter beside astrolabe)

## Universal Negative Prompts

- NO modern devices (smartphones, laptops, computer screens, LEDs, plastic)
- NO cartoon, anime, manga style
- NO sharp digital look, no 3D render smoothness
- NO neon or saturated colors
- NO text watermarks or captions
- NO cluttered UI overlays
- NO contemporary fashion
- NO obvious AI-generated artifacts (six fingers, warped faces)
