# Universe Cover · 宋（宋代文人山水）

Traditional Chinese 宋代文人山水 (Song dynasty literati landscape painting) style book cover, centered on 苏轼 (Su Shi) at Huangzhou / Red Cliffs.

## Subject

Classic "scholar on a boat" motif (like 马远《寒江独钓》). Small wooden boat drifts on moonlit Yangtze River. Lone scholar figure (Su Shi silhouette) holds a wine cup, gazing at towering Red Cliffs (赤壁) of Huangzhou. Bamboo stalks sway in foreground. Mist (烟雾) obscures the middle ground. A poem inscription area (leave blank, illegible) upper left — traditional 题跋 placement.

## Technique

**Song dynasty literati painting**:
- 马远/夏珪 "one-corner" (马一角夏半边) composition emphasis
- Ink wash with very subtle color tinting
- Sharp 斧劈皴 strokes on cliff textures
- Atmospheric mist rendered in wet brush, white space
- Distant peaks fading into 留白
- Poetic, meditative quality
- A small vermillion 印章 in corner

## Composition

**2:3 portrait, painting fills canvas edge-to-edge**. NO Western frame, NO Art Deco border, NO archival paper. High-angle view with significant 留白 (empty space) in upper portion. Boat and scholar in lower third, cliffs rising on one side.

## Palette

Ink black dominant on cliffs and trees. Pale teal river. Tarnished gold moon only. Ivory mist. Touch of oxblood on scholar's robe. Overall restrained Song literati palette.

## Mood

Contemplative, melancholic, poetic solitude of a exiled scholar. "大江东去" mood from 苏轼's 《念奴娇·赤壁怀古》.

## Negative prompts

NO Western Art Deco frames, NO Art Nouveau, NO oval photo, NO aged sepia photograph, NO archival paper border, NO Western decorative elements, NO realistic photo, NO 3D render. Must be Song-era Chinese literati ink landscape filling entire canvas.
